# Greenlatch — positioning

## 1. Competitive alternatives

What would the customer use if this offer did not exist? Include the status quo,
not just direct rivals.

- Asking a son, a daughter or a neighbour to mow and cut back · A landscaping firm
  that only takes large jobs · Letting the garden go · Paving it over

## 2. Unique attributes

What do you have that those alternatives do not? Capabilities, assets, model —
facts, not adjectives.

- The same gardener on every visit where the rota allows · A visit every second
  week on a weekday agreed at the start of the season · One fixed monthly price for
  the whole season · Small gardens only, up to 300 square metres

## 3. Value + proof

| Unique attribute | Value it enables | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| The same gardener | Someone they know at the gate, not a stranger | 9 gardeners; 94% of visits in 2025 made by the customer's regular gardener |
| A visit every second week, on an agreed day | The garden never gets away from them | 4,100 visits in 2025; 97% on the agreed day |
| One fixed monthly price | No bill to worry about | No price changed mid-season for any of 310 customers in 2025 |
| Small gardens only | Their garden is the right size of job, not a favour | 310 gardens in 2025, the largest 290 square metres |

## 4. Target customer

- **Best-fit customer:** homeowners over 70 with a small garden they have looked
  after themselves for decades
- **Why they care most:** the garden is their pride and it is slipping; asking the
  family for help feels like being a burden
- **Where they are:** the suburbs of one mid-size city; found through neighbours
  and the parish newsletter

## 5. Market category

- **Market category:** garden care for older homeowners
- **Trap test:** "garden maintenance" returns landscaping firms and DIY guides; safe
- **Familiar frame, as the foil (optional):** the landscaping firm

## Positioning statement (one paragraph)

> For homeowners over 70 who are proud of a garden they can no longer manage alone,
> Greenlatch is a garden care service that comes every second week at one fixed
> monthly price, with the customer's regular gardener where the rota allows,
> unlike asking the family or hiring a landscaping firm that only wants large jobs,
> because the whole service is built around small gardens: 4,100 visits in 2025,
> 97% on the agreed day, 94% by the regular gardener.

- **Human version (optional):** It is still your garden. We do the heavy part,
  nearly always on the day we agreed and with a face you know at the gate.
- **Core positioning term:** garden care for older homeowners
- **One-line boilerplate (≤ 12 words):** Garden care for older homeowners, with a regular gardener.
- **~50-word boilerplate:** Greenlatch looks after small gardens for homeowners over 70.
  A gardener comes every second week for one fixed monthly price through the season;
  in 2025, 94% of visits were made by the customer's regular gardener and 97% fell
  on the agreed day. You keep the garden you are proud of, and nobody in the
  family has to give up a Saturday for it.
