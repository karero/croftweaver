# Bäckerei Example — positioning

## 1. Competitive alternatives

What would the customer use if this offer did not exist? Include the status quo,
not just direct rivals.

- The supermarket's bake-off counter (warm, cheap, the flour's origin unknown) · A
  bakery chain's branch · Baking sourdough at home on a Sunday · Going without good
  bread on weekdays

## 2. Unique attributes

What do you have that those alternatives do not? Capabilities, assets, model —
facts, not adjectives.

- Flour from two named mills within 60 km, each sack traceable to the field · A
  sourdough fed daily since 2011 · Every loaf rests 24 hours before it is baked ·
  The day's bake list is posted by 6 am, and a loaf can be set aside until 6 pm

## 3. Value + proof

| Unique attribute | Value it enables | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Traceable flour from two mills | The customer knows what is in the bread | The mill and the field are named on each day's bake list |
| 24-hour rest | Bread that keeps for five days and is easy to digest | Long fermentation; the same recipe since 2011 |
| Loaf set aside until 6 pm | Good bread on a working day, not only on Saturday | About 140 loaves set aside each week in 2025 |

## 4. Target customer

- **Best-fit customer:** people in Leipzig's Südvorstadt and Connewitz who cook at
  home and care where their food comes from
- **Why they care most:** they read labels, and a loaf with a list of additives or no
  origin at all is one they would rather not buy
- **Where they are:** on foot or by bike within fifteen minutes of the shop; they hear
  of it from neighbours and at the weekly market

## 5. Market category

- **Market category:** sourdough bakery
- **Trap test:** "sourdough bakery Leipzig" returns bakeries and their opening hours; safe
- **Familiar frame, as the foil (optional):** the bakery chain on the corner

## Positioning statement (one paragraph)

> For people in Leipzig who care where their bread's flour comes from, Bäckerei Example
> is a sourdough bakery that bakes with traceable flour from two nearby mills and lets
> every loaf rest for 24 hours, unlike the supermarket's bake-off counter or a chain's
> branch, because the mill and the field are named on each day's bake list.
