## Brief

**Page type.** An article that answers a question, a home page, a product or service page, or a
contact or legal page. The review judges depth and FAQ by this.

**Reader.** Who reads this, and what do they already know? (One or two sentences.)

**Main query.** What they type into a search box or ask an AI. Then two or three related ones.
<!-- Example, made up: "how to keep a sourdough starter alive"; related: "sourdough starter
smells like nail polish", "revive a neglected starter". -->

**After reading.** What the reader should do next (a link, a form, a download).

**Title, H1, description.** Led by the search words. Keep the site's limits (`website-seo-geo`).

**Original angle.** What this page says that the top results do not, in one sentence. Say which
kind it is: a provocative point, our own experience or process, or an outlook on where this is
going.

**Outline.** One line per section, and what the reader must learn from it.

**Graphics.** Which idea needs a picture, and whether it is a simple diagram or a richer
illustration. "None" is an answer; say why.

**FAQ.** Four to six questions. Each adds something the body does not say.

**Inputs.** Every document, link or note the owner supplied, each marked **source** or **ideas
only**. Ideas-only input may suggest questions to research. It never backs a claim, a citation or
a link unless the owner reclassifies it after the source has been read.

**Claims.** One row per claim a reader could check.

| Claim | Source (a page the reader can open) | Owner sign-off needed? |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

**Outbound links.** Each link is a source, a neutral reference or an endorsement. A commercial
site needs the owner's sign-off.

| Link | Kind | Owner sign-off |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

**What we will not say**, and why. (A line per item: the claim, and the reason.)

**Proof the reader can see.** Every proof point is one the reader can open or understand without
access. No internal ticket, pull request or repository names on a public page.

**Research file.** Path to the saved research (top results, an AI answer, what they cover and
miss). The content review reads it with this document.
