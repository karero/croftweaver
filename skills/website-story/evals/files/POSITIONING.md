# Spoke & Go — positioning

## 1. Competitive alternatives

What would the customer use if this offer did not exist? Include the status quo,
not just direct rivals.

- The big chain bike store (drop the bike off, wait in a queue of about two weeks)
  · Fixing it themselves on a weekend · Riding it as it is, with the brakes that
  squeal and the gears that slip · Taking the car or the tram instead

## 2. Unique attributes

What do you have that those alternatives do not? Capabilities, assets, model —
facts, not adjectives.

- Pickup and return at home or at the office, in a two-hour window the customer
  books · Repaired within 48 hours of pickup · A fixed price quoted after a free
  check and agreed before any work starts · A loan bike for the days without one

## 3. Value + proof

| Unique attribute | Value it enables | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Pickup and return, booked window | The commuter never loses a working day to the repair | 2,300 pickups in 2025; 96% within the booked window |
| Repaired within 48 hours | The bike is back before the next week starts | Median turnaround 31 hours in 2025 |
| Fixed price agreed before work | No surprise on the bill | Price changed after agreement on 0 of 2,300 jobs in 2025 |
| Loan bike | Still riding while the bike is in the workshop | 40 loan bikes; used on 61% of jobs |

## 4. Target customer

- **Best-fit customer:** people who commute by bike at least three days a week and
  work full days, in one mid-size city
- **Why they care most:** a broken bike means a week of car, tram or late arrivals,
  and a workshop visit costs them the very hours they do not have
- **Where they are:** city centre and business parks; found through employers' bike
  schemes and through colleagues

## 5. Market category

- **Market category:** bike repair with pickup and return
- **Trap test:** "bike repair" returns local workshops and DIY guides; safe
- **Familiar frame, as the foil (optional):** the bike shop down the road

## Positioning statement (one paragraph)

> For people who commute by bike and cannot lose a working day to a repair, Spoke & Go
> is a bike repair service with pickup and return that collects the bike at home or at
> work and brings it back within 48 hours at a price agreed up front, unlike the chain
> store's two-week queue or a weekend of fixing it yourself, because the whole service
> runs around the commuter's booked window: 2,300 pickups in 2025, 96% on time.

- **Human version (optional):** Your bike breaks, your week does not. We pick it up,
  fix it, and bring it back before you need it again.
- **Core positioning term:** bike repair with pickup
- **One-line boilerplate (≤ 12 words):** Bike repair that comes to you, back within 48 hours.
- **~50-word boilerplate:** Spoke & Go repairs commuters' bikes without costing them a
  working day. We collect the bike at home or at the office in a window you book, fix it
  within 48 hours at a price agreed before we start, and bring it back. A loan bike keeps
  you riding in between.
